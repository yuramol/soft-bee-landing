SoftBee Smart Estimation — sample project brief (manual test)

Project name: Atlas Ops Console
Client type: Mid-market B2B SaaS
Goal: Build an internal operations console that lets customer success and ops teams
manage accounts, tickets, and billing signals in one place.

Problem
Today the team jumps between Notion, spreadsheets, Stripe dashboards, and a legacy
admin panel. That slows response times and creates inconsistent account notes.

Must-have features
1. Secure login with email/password and Google SSO
2. Role-based access: Admin, Success Manager, Viewer
3. Account list with search, filters (plan, health score, region), and pagination
4. Account detail page: overview, contacts, open tickets, recent activity timeline
5. Ticket inbox: create/assign/close tickets, priority, SLA due dates
6. Stripe webhook ingestion for payment failed / subscription canceled events
7. Basic audit log for sensitive actions (role changes, exports)
8. CSV export for accounts and tickets

Nice-to-have
- In-app notifications and email digests
- Saved filter views per user
- Light/dark theme

Technical preferences
- Next.js App Router + TypeScript
- Supabase for auth + Postgres + RLS
- Deploy on Vercel
- Background jobs for webhook processing

Integrations
- Stripe (webhooks + customer lookup)
- Google Workspace SSO
- Optional Slack notifications for high-priority tickets

Non-functional requirements
- Target launch in 10–14 weeks
- Support ~50 internal users at launch, grow to ~200
- P95 page load under 2s on broadband
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA for core flows
- Audit-friendly logging retained for 1 year

Out of scope for v1
- Customer-facing portal
- Native mobile apps
- Full BI/reporting warehouse
- Multi-tenant white-label

Acceptance criteria for estimate
Please return a preliminary hours range and budget range for design + engineering,
including QA and a short discovery/setup buffer. Call out major risks (SSO, Stripe
webhooks, RLS complexity) if they materially affect the estimate.

Sample user story
As a Success Manager, I want to open an account and see unpaid invoices and open
tickets together so I can prioritize outreach before renewal.
